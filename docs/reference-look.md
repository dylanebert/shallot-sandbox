# The archive as a lighting reference

Reference: Shallot `archive/sandbox-avbd`, extracted with `git archive` into `/tmp/sandbox-archive`, not added as a worktree. `bun install` resolved the sandbox's file-linked AVBD package; `bun run build` built the archive's audio, tooling and physics kernels. A temporary Playwright subject there builds the actual sandbox manifest and advances `State.step` with a GPU fence between frames. The room and hall cameras are at `[0, 1.6, 3]` and `[0, 1.6, -8]`, with 75° FOV; the held view uses yaw .03 and pitch -.1. Both sets use a 1280×720 viewport and 360-line nearest-sampled rendering. AVBD and modern standard physics do not settle loose props to identical poses.

The archive PNGs are `/tmp/sandbox-captures/archive-{room,hall-fog-on,hall-fog-off,held-block}.png`. Current captures use the same names without `archive-`. The archive defaults fog off, so its room and held captures are clear; the current game's requested desktop default remains on. The hall pair isolates fog in both engines.

## Translation, not numeric copying

The modern precedents are `examples/lit-interior` for lumen-based lamps and ambient fill, `examples/day-night-cycle` and Fog JSDoc for physical extinction/scattering, `examples/post-processing` and camera grading JSDoc for presentation, and scream-jam's standard-derived OKLab material for banding.

| Difference before this revision | Cause and current translation |
| --- | --- |
| Narrow ceiling hotspot, insufficient fill on shadowed faces | Archive `lit` multiplies albedo by unitless light factors, with wrapped diffuse `(N·L/2+.5)^2`. Current standard uses Lambertian diffuse and exposure. At default EV100 9.7, exposure is `2^-9.7 / 1.2 ≈ 1/998`; converting old intensity 78 to unwrapped physical flux would give `78 × 4π² / exposure ≈ 3.07M lm`. Use 1.5M lm direct plus more warm fill, rather than pretending this also recreates wrapped diffuse. |
| Near-black held face and ceiling | Archive ambient .8 is unexposed white radiance. Its nominal modern equivalent is ~800 cd/m². Current fill is 1300 cd/m², `#fff0da`: a modest warm extra replaces some wrapped light lost on grazing/shadowed faces. This is deliberately not exact angular equivalence. |
| Inappropriate identical architecture albedo | Restore the reference sRGB palette, decoded to linear: floor `#2b2928`, ceiling `#4c4b49`, walls `#393836`, pit `#1e1d1b`. Previously every surface used linear `.115,.108,.098`, making walls and especially the floor too bright/grey relative to the ceiling. Wood, links, planks, fixed mounts and weight also regain their separate reference colors. |
| Architecture lacked weathered mottling | World-space value noise now modulates albedo at broad, medium and tooth scales. This remains continuous across architectural boxes. The material is rebuilt, not the archive's old surface shader transplanted. |
| Shadows too severe on dark faces | Stronger warm ambient lifts shadow floors without disabling shadow maps. Both lamps retain local point shadows; modern Gaussian filtering is retained rather than reimplementing the archive's 3×3 PCF. Shadow shape and softness therefore are not pixel-identical. |
| Haze/halo did not resemble the archive | Archive density .03 directly controls extinction; its absorption .1 is a *fraction*, while `scattering × scatterIntensity = 4 × .4 = 1.6` is an independent shaft gain. Modern coefficients are instead density .03, absorption .1, scattering .9: extinction remains .03 and scattering albedo .9. Keep height falloff .05 and asymmetry .5. Modern fog also scatters ambient fill, unlike the old color-only analytic background term; lightIntensity .3 balances that extra source against the archive halo/depth readback. Fog's color remains `#2a2016`. |
| Frame was too neutral and its blacks crushed differently | The archive uses Khronos Neutral, RGB CDL slope `[1.06,1,.94]`, offset -.01, power 1.12, saturation .92. Current tonemapping explicitly selects Khronos PBR Neutral; grading uses reciprocal gamma `1/1.12`, lift -.01, saturation .92 and a .012 warm white-point shift. Current grading's tonal-section vectors are **not RGB CDL vectors**, so temperature is the idiomatic approximation of the warm slope, not a one-to-one CDL port. The earlier contrast around .5 crushed low radiance excessively. |
| Smooth surfaces, no stepped lightness | All three surfaces now extend standard Materials and run `applyPbrLighting(pbrInputFromStandardMaterial(ctx))`, then quantize OKLab L to ten bands. Timber UVs are code-authored in metres along the longest axis so the standard-derived shader needs no private transform bindings. Fog remains smooth. |

## Remaining presentation differences

- The archive radius 4 was a deliberately nonphysical near-field diffuse clamp. Modern radius describes the spherical emitter. The modern source uses the visible bulb's .15 m radius, not a 4 m sphere intersecting the architecture. Together with the unwrapped diffuse response, this leaves a more local ceiling highlight than the archive. Lamps sit .25 m below the inner ceiling face, avoiding a source on its shadow boundary. Broad *fill* is provided by ambient, not an emulated legacy attenuation shader.
- The archive applies banding **after** grade, tonemap, saturation and fog; this game's requested standard-material shape applies banding **before** fog and tonemap. The bands will not land on identical display RGB values. The material's ten-band math is tested independently in floating-point GPU readback.
- Ordered Bayer 4×4 dither is pending a published `ctx.fragCoord`. Its GPU TODO states the adjacent-band alternation claim; executing it with `--todo` reaches the explicit missing-operation error. No private engine import or object-UV substitute is used. Without it, band boundaries and noise islands remain visibly harder than the archive. Camera DebandDither is disabled: display-code noise is not a substitute for one-band OKLab Bayer noise.
- The slight vignette remains extras' HDR Vignette at intensity .15. The archive applies a gamma-compensated vignette after banding; extras' HDR placement/curve is not identical. Both remain subtle rather than a custom post-process workaround.
- Resolution remains 360 lines, point-upsampled, without MSAA. HUD remains full-resolution DOM, included over the captured target.

## Evidence

`src/frame.node.ts` reads the full game's texture through public APIs and toggles G through engine input. The halo difference minimum (15 display-code levels) and cast-shadow pixel control (>100 pixels brightened by >25 red levels when shadow maps are disabled) are unchanged. The old absolute **floor** contrast check is replaced by a **wall-pool** contrast ratio of at least 1.25 against ambient-only rendering: the reference floor albedo is far darker, and material-stage ten-band quantization invalidates the old absolute +25 floor criterion. This is a changed observable, not a silently lowered floor threshold.

`src/posterize.gpu.test.ts` renders the exact material helper on a fullscreen pass to rgba32float and reads back the expected RGB cube of each quantized neutral OKLab level, including endpoints. It also records the pending Bayer claim. The GPU tier is included in CI alongside the existing full-composition Node tier.
